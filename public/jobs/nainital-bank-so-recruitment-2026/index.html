👨‍💻 How to Apply

  • Registration:

    Visit the Nainital Bank official website, open Recruitment → Current Openings → Apply Online, click New Registration, and enter your name, mobile number and email ID to generate the Registration Number and Password.

  • Application Details:

    Login with your Registration Number and Password, enter your personal details, category, address and other required information, and select only one eligible Specialist Officer post.

  • Qualification & Experience:

    Enter your educational qualifications and post-qualification work experience according to the eligibility requirements of the selected post.

  • Examination Centre:

    Select three preferred examination centres from Haldwani, Dehradun, Lucknow and Delhi/NCR.

  • Upload Documents:

    Upload your recent photograph, signature, left thumb impression and handwritten declaration in the prescribed format, size and dimensions; also complete the required live photograph capture.

  • Preview & Registration:

    Preview the complete application carefully, verify all details and uploaded documents, then click Complete Registration; changes will not be allowed after final submission.

  • Fee Payment:

    Click the Payment tab and pay the ₹1,500/- application fee including GST through the available online payment options, then complete the submission.

  • Print Application:

    After successful submission and payment, download the e-Receipt and final application form and take a printout for future reference.
