Azim Premji Foundation Scholarship 2026
The Azim Premji Scholarship 2026 is a flagship initiative of the Azim Premji Foundation aimed at supporting girl students from disadvantaged backgrounds to continue their higher education. Under this program, eligible students receive ₹30,000 per year for the entire duration of their first undergraduate degree or diploma course (2–5 years) at recognized government or private colleges across India. To qualify, the applicant must have passed Class 10 and 12 as a regular student from a government school or college and secured admission in the first year of a UG or diploma course for the 2025–26 academic session. The scholarship is open to students from select states and union territories including Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Rajasthan, Sikkim, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, and Uttarakhand. The application process for the Scholarship Cohort 2025 will begin on 10 September 2025, and candidates must submit documents like a passport-size photograph, Aadhaar card, and signature image. Importantly, there is no application fee, and students are advised to beware of fraud or impostors.

Samagra Portal 2026 - Download Samagra ID
The MP Samagra ID Portal (samagra.gov.in) is an official initiative by the Department of Social Justice and Disabled Welfare, Government of Madhya Pradesh. It helps citizens register their families and members under a single database to avail benefits of various state welfare schemes. Through this portal, users can easily apply for a new Samagra ID, print Family or Member ID cards, and update personal details such as name, address, or Aadhaar information online. This digital system ensures transparency, quick access, and smooth delivery of government services to every eligible household in Madhya Pradesh.
