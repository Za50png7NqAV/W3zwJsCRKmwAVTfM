NTA CSIR UGC NET December 2026 Online Form

NTA UGC NET JRF December 2026 Online Form
UGC NET December 2026 is conducted for eligibility for Junior Research Fellowship (JRF), Assistant Professor and Ph.D. admission. Online applications are scheduled from 08/10/2026 to 28/10/2026, with the examination scheduled from 14/12/2026 to 19/12/2026. Candidates generally need a Master’s degree with 55% marks, while OBC-NCL, SC, ST, PwD/PwBD and Third Gender candidates require 50% marks.
