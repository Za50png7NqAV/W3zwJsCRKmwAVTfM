ISRO Assistant & Junior Personal Assistant Vacancy 2026
The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has released the ISRO Recruitment 2026 Notification through the ISRO Centralised Recruitment Board (ICRB) for 242 vacancies of Assistant, Upper Division Clerk (UDC), Junior Personal Assistant (JPA), and Stenographer across various ISRO Centres and Autonomous Bodies in India. Candidates with a Graduate degree (minimum 60% marks) or the prescribed qualification for JPA/Stenographer posts are eligible to apply. Selected candidates will receive a starting basic pay of ₹25,500 (Level-4 Pay Matrix) along with DA, HRA, TA, and other admissible allowances as per Government of India rules. Eligible candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully and submit their applications before the closing date.

RRB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts. Indian Overseas Bank Apprentice Vacancy 2026
Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) has released the Apprentice Recruitment 2026 Notification for 750 vacancies under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Eligible graduates can apply online from 07/07/2026 to 20/07/2026. Candidates must hold a bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university and be 20 to 28 years of age as on 01/07/2026.
