Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 6th Admission 2027
Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) 2027 is conducted by the Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) for admission to Class VI in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas for the academic session 2027–28. Eligible candidates must be bona fide residents of the concerned district, studying in Class V during the 2026–27 session in a recognized school, and born between 01 May 2015 and 31 July 2017 (both dates inclusive).
