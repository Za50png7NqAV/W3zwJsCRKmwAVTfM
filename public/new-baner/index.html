PM Internship Yojana (PMIS) 2026
Prime Minister Internship Scheme (PMIS) एक सरकारी योजना है, जिसका उद्देश्य युवाओं को कंपनियों में इंटर्नशिप के माध्यम से प्रैक्टिकल अनुभव और स्किल डेवलपमेंट प्रदान करना है। यह योजना Ministry of Corporate Affairs द्वारा संचालित की जाती है, जिसमें युवाओं को 6–9 महीने की इंटर्नशिप, ₹9000 तक मासिक स्टाइपेंड, और अनुभव प्रमाण पत्र दिया जाता है। इस योजना के तहत चयनित उम्मीदवारों को Top Companies में काम सीखने का अवसर मिलता है, जिससे उनकी रोजगार क्षमता बढ़ती है और भविष्य में नौकरी मिलने की संभावना मजबूत होती है।

UP Police Constable Result 2026
UP Police has released the Constable Recruitment 2025 notification for 32,679 vacancies across multiple units such as Civil Police, PAC, Special Security Force, Female Battalion, Mounted Police and Jail Warder. Applicants must have passed Intermediate (12th) from a recognized board. The prescribed age limit is 18 to 22 years for male candidates and 18 to 25 years for female candidates, with category-wise relaxation as per government norms. The recruitment process consists of a written test followed by PST, PET, document verification and medical examination. The online application window will be open from 31/12/2025 to 30/01/2026, making it one of the largest police recruitments in Uttar Pradesh.

RRB Group D / Level 1 CEN 09/2025 Admit Card 2026
Railway Recruitment Boards have released the indicative notice for Level-1 / Group D Recruitment under CEN 09/2025 to fill around 21997 vacancies across various RRB regions. These posts fall under Level-1 of the 7th Pay Commission with a starting pay of ₹18000 per month along with allowances. The online application process will begin on 21 January 2026 and will remain open till 20 February 2026 on the official RRB application portal. Candidates between 18 and 33 years of age as on 01 January 2026 who meet the prescribed educational qualifications will be eligible to apply. Complete details regarding post-wise eligibility selection process syllabus and medical standards will be available in the detailed notification published by the respective RRBs.

BPSSC Bihar ASI Technical Exam Postponed 2026
Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has invited online applications for Assistant Sub Inspector (Technical) Recruitment 2026 in Bihar Police Radio Organization. UP Anganwadi Worker Bharti 2026
UP Anganwadi Bharti 2026 is a recruitment drive conducted by the Child Development Services & Nutrition Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh, to fill Anganwadi Worker (कार्यकर्त्री) vacancies across various districts. Eligible female candidates who have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) and are permanent residents of the concerned Village/Ward/Nyaya Panchayat can apply online. Candidates should check the district-wise notification for vacancy details, eligibility, important dates, and the application process before submitting their online application.
