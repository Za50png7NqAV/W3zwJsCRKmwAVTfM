MP Rojgar Panjiyan Online Registration 2026
MP Rojgar Panjiyan 2026 is the official employment registration system launched by the Government of Madhya Pradesh. Through this portal (mprojgar.gov.in), job seekers can register for free, get notified about government and private job opportunities, participate in job fairs, and renew their registration every 3 years. The process is fully online, and applicants need to provide basic personal information, educational documents, and Aadhaar details to complete their profile.

MPESB Candidate Profiling / Profile Panjiyan Form Online
To apply for any government recruitment under the Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB), all candidates must complete the Candidate Profile Registration process. This is a one-time mandatory step where applicants register using Aadhaar eKYC, upload personal and academic details, and receive a unique Profile ID. This ID is required for all future MPESB applications. The process is simple, online, and essential for anyone seeking government jobs in Madhya Pradesh.

UPSC IES / ISS Result 2026
Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has released the notification for Indian Economic Service (IES) and Indian Statistical Service (ISS) Examination 2026. A total of 44 vacancies (16 for IES and 28 for ISS) are available. Eligible candidates can apply online from 11/02/2026 to 03/03/2026. The written examination will commence from 19/06/2026. Candidates must meet the prescribed educational qualifications in Economics or Statistics as per service requirement. The selection process includes a written examination followed by an interview.

MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 4 Patwari and Other Vacancy 2026
The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the Group 2 Sub Group 4 Combined Recruitment Test 2026 notification to fill 2,106 vacancies across various government departments in Madhya Pradesh. The online application process begins on 04/08/2026 and will remain open until 18/08/2026, while application corrections can be made up to 23/08/2026. The recruitment examination is scheduled to start from 22/09/2026. Candidates possessing a Bachelor's Degree or other post-specific qualifications from a recognized university are eligible to apply, subject to the eligibility criteria prescribed for each post. Applicants are advised to read the official notification carefully before submitting their online application.
