RRB Group D / Level 1 CEN 09/2025 Admit Card 2026
Railway Recruitment Boards have released the indicative notice for Level-1 / Group D Recruitment under CEN 09/2025 to fill around 21997 vacancies across various RRB regions. These posts fall under Level-1 of the 7th Pay Commission with a starting pay of ₹18000 per month along with allowances. The online application process will begin on 21 January 2026 and will remain open till 20 February 2026 on the official RRB application portal. Candidates between 18 and 33 years of age as on 01 January 2026 who meet the prescribed educational qualifications will be eligible to apply. Complete details regarding post-wise eligibility selection process syllabus and medical standards will be available in the detailed notification published by the respective RRBs.

UPSSSC Lower PCS Pre Admit Card 2026
UPSSSC Lower Subordinate Recruitment 2026 is a state-level government job notification released for 2285 posts under various departments of Uttar Pradesh. The online application process starts from 29 May 2026 and the last date to apply is 18 June 2026. Candidates must have a graduation degree and a valid UPSSSC PET 2025 score card to be eligible. The application fee is ₹25 for all categories, and both male and female candidates can apply.

RRB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts. A total of 4098 vacancies will be filled across various RRBs in India. Eligible candidates will be able to submit their applications online from 14/08/2026 (Tentative) to 13/09/2026 (Tentative). Selected candidates will receive an initial pay of ₹35,400 per month under Pay Level-6 (7th CPC). Applicants must be 18 to 33 years of age as on 01/01/2027, and the detailed notification will include post-wise eligibility, selection process, application fee, and other recruitment details. Candidates are advised to keep their Aadhaar details updated before applying online.

BPSC School Teacher TRE 4.0 Vacancy 2026
Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 notification for the recruitment of 32,388 School Teachers under the Education Department, Bihar. Vacancies are available for Primary (Classes 1–5), Middle (Classes 6–8), Secondary (Classes 9–10) and Higher Secondary (Classes 11–12) teachers. Eligible candidates can apply online from 01/09/2026 to 30/09/2026, with fee payment available from 01/09/2026 to 29/09/2026. Candidates should check the prescribed educational qualifications, subject-wise eligibility, age limit and other conditions before applying.
