UP Anganwadi Worker Bharti 2026
UP Anganwadi Bharti 2026 is a recruitment drive conducted by the Child Development Services & Nutrition Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh, to fill Anganwadi Worker (कार्यकर्त्री) vacancies across various districts. Eligible female candidates who have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) and are permanent residents of the concerned Village/Ward/Nyaya Panchayat can apply online. Candidates should check the district-wise notification for vacancy details, eligibility, important dates, and the application process before submitting their online application.

Union Bank Specialist Officer SO Admit Card 2026
Union Bank of India has released the Union Bank Recruitment Project 2026-27 notification for the recruitment of 395 regular vacancies in General Banking Officers, Officers with Domain Expertise, and Specialist Officer (SO) posts. The online application process will commence on 21 July 2026 and continue until 10 August 2026. Candidates possessing qualifications such as Graduate, B.E./B.Tech, MBA, MCA, CA, LLB, M.Tech, and other relevant professional degrees, along with the required post-wise experience, are eligible to apply. The selection process may include an Online Examination, Group Discussion (if applicable), Personal Interview, Document Verification, and Medical Examination. Interested candidates should carefully check the post-wise eligibility criteria before submitting the online application.
