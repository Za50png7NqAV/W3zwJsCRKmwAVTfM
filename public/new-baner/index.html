Indian Air Force Agniveervayu Musician Vacancy 2026
The Indian Air Force Agniveervayu (Musician) Intake 01/2027 recruitment invites online applications from unmarried Indian male and female candidates from all States and Union Territories of India. Candidates who have passed Matriculation (10th) and possess the required musical proficiency and a valid music qualification/certificate can apply. The online registration process will be held from 20 July 2026 to 02 August 2026, and the recruitment rally is scheduled from 31 August 2026 to 09 September 2026. The selection process includes document verification, Physical Fitness Test (PFT), English written test, Music Proficiency Test, and Medical Examination. Selected candidates will be enrolled as Agniveervayu (Musician) under the Agnipath Scheme for a period of four years, with salary, allowances, insurance, and Seva Nidhi benefits as per Indian Air Force rules.

RRC NFR Apprentice Vacancy 2026
The Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), Northeast Frontier Railway (NFR) has released the Apprentices Recruitment 2026 notification for 6,777 Act Apprentice vacancies in various trades under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Eligible candidates who have passed Class 10 with ITI (NCVT/SCVT) in the relevant trade can apply online. For Medical Laboratory Technician trades, candidates must have passed 10th and 12th (PCB) with the required marks. The online application process will begin on 20/07/2026 and continue until 19/08/2026. Selection will be based on a merit list prepared from Matriculation and ITI marks, followed by Document Verification and Medical Examination. Interested candidates should read the official notification carefully before applying.
