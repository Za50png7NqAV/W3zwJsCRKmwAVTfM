UPESSC Assistant Professor Vacancy 2026
UPESSC has released the Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 notification for 1,936 posts in Uttar Pradesh. Eligible candidates can apply online through the OTR-based application process from 08/09/2026 to 07/10/2026. The recruitment includes 1,564 posts in co-education colleges and 372 posts in women’s colleges. Candidates generally need a Master’s degree in the relevant subject with at least 55% marks, along with NET/SLET/SET or applicable Ph.D. exemption as prescribed in the notification. The written examination is scheduled for 19/11/2026 and 20/11/2026, followed by an interview. The selection process carries 400 marks, including 360 marks for the written exam and 40 marks for the interview.
