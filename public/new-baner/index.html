BPSSC Forest Range Officer (FRO) Vacancy 2026
The Bihar Police Subordinate Services Commission (BPSSC) has released the Forest Range Officer (FRO) Recruitment 2026 Notification (Advt. No. 10/2026) for 16 vacancies in the Environment, Forest & Climate Change Department, Government of Bihar. Eligible Indian candidates possessing a Bachelor's Degree in Agriculture, Forestry, Botany, Zoology, Veterinary Science, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Statistics, Geology, Engineering, or other prescribed disciplines can apply online from 16/07/2026 to 16/08/2026. The selection process consists of a Written Examination, Interview, Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), and Medical Examination. Candidates should carefully read the official notification to verify their eligibility, physical standards, and other recruitment conditions before submitting the online application.

RRB Group D / Level 1 CEN 09/2025 Application Stauts 2026
Railway Recruitment Boards have released the indicative notice for Level-1 / Group D Recruitment under CEN 09/2025 to fill around 21997 vacancies across various RRB regions. These posts fall under Level-1 of the 7th Pay Commission with a starting pay of ₹18000 per month along with allowances. The online application process will begin on 21 January 2026 and will remain open till 20 February 2026 on the official RRB application portal. Candidates between 18 and 33 years of age as on 01 January 2026 who meet the prescribed educational qualifications will be eligible to apply. Complete details regarding post-wise eligibility selection process syllabus and medical standards will be available in the detailed notification published by the respective RRBs.

UPSC CAPF Assistant Commandant Admit Card 2026
The CAPF Assistant Commandant 2026 Notification has been officially released for a total of 349 vacancies in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, and SSB. The online application process started on 20/02/2026 and the last date to apply is 12/03/2026 (till 6:00 PM). The written examination will be conducted on 19/07/2026. Candidates must hold a Bachelor’s Degree from a recognized university. The age limit is 20 to 25 years as on 01 August 2026, with age relaxation applicable as per government rules for reserved categories. The selection process includes Written Examination, Physical Standards Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Interview/Personality Test, and Medical Examination. Both male and female candidates are eligible to apply.
