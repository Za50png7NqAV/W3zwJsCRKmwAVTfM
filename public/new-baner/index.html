IAF Airmen Group Y Medical Assistant Intake 02/2027
Indian Air Force (IAF) has released the Medical Assistant Recruitment 2026 notification for Airmen Group ‘Y’ Intake 02/2027. Eligible male Indian and Gorkha candidates can apply online from 06 July 2026 to 26 July 2026. Candidates must have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology and English or possess a Diploma/B.Pharm/B.Sc in Pharmacy with the required marks. The selection process includes an Online Test, Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Adaptability Tests, and Medical Examination. Selected candidates will receive a stipend of ₹14,600 during training and a starting salary of ₹26,900 per month after successful completion of training. The online examination will be conducted on 22 and 23 September 2026.

Indian Air Force Agniveervayu Intake 02/2027 Online Form
Indian Air Force (IAF) has released the Agniveervayu Intake 02/2027 Notification under the Agnipath Scheme for unmarried male and female candidates. Eligible candidates can submit online applications from 06 July 2026 to 26 July 2026, while the online examination will be conducted on 22–23 September 2026. Candidates who have passed 12th (Science/Any Stream), Engineering Diploma, or Vocational Course with the required marks can apply. The selection process includes an Online Test, Physical Fitness Test (PFT), Adaptability Test, Document Verification, and Medical Examination. Selected candidates will serve in the Indian Air Force for four years and receive an attractive salary package, allowances, insurance cover, and a Seva Nidhi package of approximately ₹10.04 lakh after completion of service. Interested candidates should carefully read the official notification before applying.

UP Anganwadi Worker Bharti 2026
UP Anganwadi Bharti 2026 is a recruitment drive conducted by the Child Development Services & Nutrition Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh, to fill Anganwadi Worker (कार्यकर्त्री) vacancies across various districts. Eligible female candidates who have passed 10+2 (Intermediate) and are permanent residents of the concerned Village/Ward/Nyaya Panchayat can apply online. Candidates should check the district-wise notification for vacancy details, eligibility, important dates, and the application process before submitting their online application.

SBI Circle Based Officer CBO Interview Letter 2026
SBI CBO Recruitment 2026 has been announced by the State Bank of India for 2050 Circle Based Officer (CBO) posts. The online application process will be open from 29 January 2026 to 18 February 2026. Candidates must hold a graduation degree in any discipline and have at least 2 years of experience as an Officer in a Scheduled Commercial Bank or Regional Rural Bank (RRB). The selection process includes an online examination, screening of experience, interview, and local language proficiency test. Selected candidates will be posted in the same circle for which they apply, making this recruitment a great opportunity for experienced banking professionals to join SBI as officers.
