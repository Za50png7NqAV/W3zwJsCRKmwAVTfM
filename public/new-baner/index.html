RRB Junior Engineer CEN 04/2026 Revised Exam Date
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts. A total of 4098 vacancies will be filled across various RRBs in India. Eligible candidates will be able to submit their applications online from 14/08/2026 (Tentative) to 13/09/2026 (Tentative). Candidates are advised to keep their Aadhaar details updated before applying online.
