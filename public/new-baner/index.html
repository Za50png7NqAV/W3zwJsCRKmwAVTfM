कल्याणी सहायता योजना (Kalayani Sahayata Yojana) 2026
कल्याणी सहायता योजना मध्य प्रदेश श्रम कल्याण बोर्ड द्वारा संचालित एक कल्याणकारी योजना है, जिसका उद्देश्य पंजीकृत औद्योगिक श्रमिक की मृत्यु के बाद उसकी पात्र विधवा को आर्थिक सहायता प्रदान करना है। इस योजना के तहत लाभार्थी को हर छह महीने में ₹6,000, अर्थात ₹12,000 प्रति वर्ष की सहायता राशि दी जाती है। आवेदन कर्मचारी की मृत्यु के एक वर्ष के भीतर करना आवश्यक है तथा सहायता जारी रखने के लिए प्रत्येक वर्ष यह प्रमाणित करना होता है कि विधवा जीवित है और उसने पुनर्विवाह नहीं किया है। योजना का लाभ प्राप्त करने के लिए मृत श्रमिक का मध्य प्रदेश श्रम कल्याण बोर्ड में पंजीकृत होना और निर्धारित पात्रता शर्तों का पूरा होना अनिवार्य है।

Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) 2026
The Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) 2026 is being conducted by the University of Kota to determine the eligibility of candidates for the post of Assistant Professor in Rajasthan. Online applications will be accepted from 14 June 2026 to 15 July 2026, and the examination is scheduled to be held on 06 September 2026. Candidates must possess a Master's Degree with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) from a recognized university. The examination will consist of two papers carrying a total of 150 questions and 300 marks, with no negative marking. The Rajasthan SET certificate will be valid for a lifetime and can be used for Assistant Professor recruitment within the state of Rajasthan.

NTA NEET UG 2026 Re Exam Result
NEET (UG) 2026 is a national-level medical entrance examination conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) under the Ministry of Education, Government of India. The exam is mandatory for admission to undergraduate medical and allied courses including MBBS, BDS, AYUSH (BAMS, BUMS, BSMS, BHMS), BVSc & AH, and B.Sc Nursing (MNS) across India. Candidates must be at least 17 years old by 31 December 2026 and should have passed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, Biology/Biotechnology, and English. There is no upper age limit and no restriction on the number of attempts. NEET (UG) 2026 will be held on 03 May 2026 in offline mode for a duration of 3 hours and will be conducted in 13 languages. Qualifying NEET is compulsory for admission to all participating institutions.

Army SSC Tech 68th Vacancy 2026
The Indian Army SSC (Tech) Recruitment 2027 notification has been released for the 68th Short Service Commission (Technical) Men & Women Courses, offering a total of 381 vacancies (350 for Men and 31 for Women). Eligible unmarried engineering graduates can apply online through the official website. The application window for SSC (Tech) Men is from 09/07/2026 to 07/08/2026, while SSC (Tech) Women can apply from 08/07/2026 to 06/08/2026. The selection process includes shortlisting, SSB interview, medical examination, and merit list. Selected candidates will undergo 49 weeks of training at the Officers Training Academy (OTA), Gaya, Bihar, receive a ₹56,100 monthly stipend during training, and be commissioned as Lieutenants in the Indian Army.
