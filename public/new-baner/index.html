UPSSSC PET 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has released the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026 notification for candidates seeking future Group 'C' Government Jobs in Uttar Pradesh. Eligible candidates who have passed High School (Class 10) or an equivalent examination can submit their online applications from 03 August 2026 to 01 September 2026 through the official UPSSSC portal. The application correction window will remain open until 08 September 2026. The minimum age required is 18 years, while the maximum age is 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with age relaxation applicable as per government rules. PET 2026 is a qualifying examination and does not announce any specific vacancies or post names. Candidates qualifying in PET will be eligible to apply for various future UPSSSC Group 'C' recruitment notifications based on their PET score.

Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) 2026
The Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) 2026 is being conducted by the University of Kota to determine the eligibility of candidates for the post of Assistant Professor in Rajasthan. Online applications will be accepted from 14 June 2026 to 15 July 2026, and the examination is scheduled to be held on 06 September 2026. Candidates must possess a Master's Degree with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) from a recognized university. The examination will consist of two papers carrying a total of 150 questions and 300 marks, with no negative marking. The Rajasthan SET certificate will be valid for a lifetime and can be used for Assistant Professor recruitment within the state of Rajasthan.

IBPS Clerk CRP CSA-XVI Vacancy 2026
The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has released the IBPS Clerk Recruitment 2026 (CRP CSA-XVI) notification for the recruitment of Customer Service Associate (Clerk) posts in participating public sector banks. Interested candidates can apply online from 01 August 2026 to 21 August 2026. Candidates must possess a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university. The application fee is Rs. 850/- for General/OBC/EWS candidates and Rs. 175/- for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates. The detailed state-wise vacancy list will be announced separately. Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before submitting the online application.
