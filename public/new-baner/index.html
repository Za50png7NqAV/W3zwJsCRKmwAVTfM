UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Vacancy 2026
UPSSSC Veterinary Pharmacist Recruitment 2026 offers 1,308 Veterinary Pharmacist posts. The online application process will be open from 14/09/2026 to 05/10/2026. Candidates must have passed Intermediate with Biology and hold a 2 years 3 months Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy. Candidates must also have appeared in UPSSSC PET-2025 and possess a PET-2025 Score Card issued by the Commission. Selection will be through a written main examination consisting of 100 questions for 100 marks, with a duration of 2 hours and 1/4 negative marking for each incorrect answer.

MP High Court Assistant Grade 3 Vacancy 2026
MP High Court has released the Assistant Grade-III Recruitment 2026 notification for 1,174 posts. Eligible candidates must have a Graduation degree in any discipline, a valid C.P.C.T.
