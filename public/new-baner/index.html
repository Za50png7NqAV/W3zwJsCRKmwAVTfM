UPSSSC Computer Cadre (Computer Operator) Vacancy 2026
UPSSSC Computer Cadre Recruitment 2026 is being conducted for a total of 12 posts, including 11 Computer Operator Grade-A posts and 1 Assistant Computer Operator post. Eligible candidates must have a graduation degree along with a Diploma in Computer Science or NIELIT/DOEACC ‘O’ Level Certificate and the required experience in the field of Computer Applications. The online application process will be open from 07/09/2026 to 28/09/2026, while the last date for fee payment/correction is 05/10/2026. The application fee is ₹25 for all categories. Selection will be through the prescribed main examination, with candidates shortlisted on the basis of their PET-2025 normalized score.

Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) Admit Card 2026
The Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) 2026 is being conducted by the University of Kota to determine the eligibility of candidates for the post of Assistant Professor in Rajasthan. Online applications will be accepted from 14 June 2026 to 15 July 2026, and the examination is scheduled to be held on 06 September 2026. Candidates must possess a Master's Degree with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) from a recognized university. The examination will consist of two papers carrying a total of 150 questions and 300 marks, with no negative marking. The Rajasthan SET certificate will be valid for a lifetime and can be used for Assistant Professor recruitment within the state of Rajasthan.

RRB Group D / Level 1 CEN 09/2025 Answer Key 2026
Railway Recruitment Boards have released the indicative notice for Level-1 / Group D Recruitment under CEN 09/2025 to fill around 21997 vacancies across various RRB regions. UP Special TET Online Form 2026
UPESSC has released the Special Teacher Eligibility Test (Special TET) 2026 under Advertisement No. 03/2026 for eligible teachers working at the Primary and Upper Primary levels, along with Special Educators (CWSN). The online application process is based on OTR, with registration starting from 05/09/2026. The examination is scheduled for 03/11/2026. The exam will consist of 150 MCQs carrying 150 marks, with a duration of 2 hours 30 minutes and no negative marking. Candidates should carefully check the prescribed eligibility, qualification, documents and other requirements before applying.
