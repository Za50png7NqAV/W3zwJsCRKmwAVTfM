Azim Premji Foundation Scholarship 2026
The Azim Premji Scholarship 2026 is a flagship initiative of the Azim Premji Foundation aimed at supporting girl students from disadvantaged backgrounds to continue their higher education. Under this program, eligible students receive ₹30,000 per year for the entire duration of their first undergraduate degree or diploma course (2–5 years) at recognized government or private colleges across India. To qualify, the applicant must have passed Class 10 and 12 as a regular student from a government school or college and secured admission in the first year of a UG or diploma course for the 2025–26 academic session. The scholarship is open to students from select states and union territories including Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, Karnataka, Madhya Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Odisha, Puducherry, Rajasthan, Sikkim, Telangana, Tripura, Uttar Pradesh, and Uttarakhand. The application process for the Scholarship Cohort 2025 will begin on 10 September 2025, and candidates must submit documents like a passport-size photograph, Aadhaar card, and signature image. Importantly, there is no application fee, and students are advised to beware of fraud or impostors.

RRB Junior Engineer Recruitment 2026
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts. A total of 4098 vacancies will be filled across various RRBs in India. Eligible candidates will be able to submit their applications online from 14/08/2026 (Tentative) to 13/09/2026 (Tentative). Selected candidates will receive an initial pay of ₹35,400 per month under Pay Level-6 (7th CPC). Applicants must be 18 to 33 years of age as on 01/01/2027, and the detailed notification will include post-wise eligibility, selection process, application fee, and other recruitment details. Candidates are advised to keep their Aadhaar details updated before applying online.

MMSKY मुख्यमंत्री सीखो कमाओ योजना 2026
मुख्यमंत्री सीखो-कमाओ योजना (MMSKY) मध्य प्रदेश सरकार की युवाओं को उद्योग एवं व्यवसाय से जुड़ा व्यावहारिक प्रशिक्षण देने वाली योजना है। इसमें 18 से 29 वर्ष के मध्य प्रदेश के स्थानीय निवासी युवा, जिन्होंने कम से कम 12वीं, ITI, डिप्लोमा या स्नातक स्तर की शिक्षा पूरी की है, पंजीकृत प्रतिष्ठानों में On-the-Job Training (OJT) प्राप्त कर सकते हैं। प्रशिक्षण के दौरान योग्यता के अनुसार ₹8,000 से ₹10,000 तक प्रतिमाह स्टाइपेंड दिया जाता है। योजना का उद्देश्य युवाओं की रोजगार क्षमता बढ़ाना और उन्हें नियमित रोजगार के लिए आवश्यक व्यावहारिक कौशल प्रदान करना है।
