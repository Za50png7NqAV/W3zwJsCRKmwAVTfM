RRB Junior Engineer CEN 04/2026 Admit Card 2026
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts. A total of 4098 vacancies will be filled across various RRBs in India. Eligible candidates will be able to submit their applications online from 14/08/2026 (Tentative) to 13/09/2026 (Tentative). Selected candidates will receive an initial pay of ₹35,400 per month under Pay Level-6 (7th CPC). Applicants must be 18 to 33 years of age as on 01/01/2027, and the detailed notification will include post-wise eligibility, selection process, application fee, and other recruitment details. Candidates are advised to keep their Aadhaar details updated before applying online.

BOB Specialist Officer SO Admit Card 2026
Bank of Baroda has released the Specialist Officer (SO) Recruitment 2026 Notification for 206 vacancies across various departments, including Digital Banking, Facility Management, Information Security, Security, Enterprise Data Management Office, and Corporate Accounts & Taxation Planning. The online application process begins on 06 August 2026 and will remain open until 26 August 2026. Candidates with qualifications such as Graduate, B.E./B.Tech, BCA, MCA, M.Sc., CA, ICWA, MBA, and other relevant professional degrees, along with the required post-qualification work experience, are eligible to apply depending on the post. The selection process may include application screening, online test, psychometric test, group discussion, and/or interview. Interested candidates should carefully read the official notification to check the detailed post-wise eligibility criteria before submitting their online application.
