SBI Bank Medical Officer BMO Vacancy 2026
The State Bank of India (SBI) has released the Bank Medical Officer (BMO-II) Recruitment 2026 notification under the Specialist Cadre Officer (SCO) recruitment drive. A total of 35 vacancies have been announced across various SBI circles in India. Selected candidates will be appointed in Middle Management Grade Scale-II (MMGS-II) with an attractive salary package, allowances, and other benefits as per SBI rules.
