UPESSC Assistant Professor Admit Card 2026
UPESSC has released the Assistant Professor Recruitment 2026 notification for 1,936 posts in Uttar Pradesh. Eligible candidates can apply online through the OTR-based application process from 08/09/2026 to 07/10/2026. The recruitment includes 1,564 posts in co-education colleges and 372 posts in women’s colleges. Candidates generally need a Master’s degree in the relevant subject with at least 55% marks, along with NET/SLET/SET or applicable Ph.D. exemption as prescribed in the notification. The written examination is scheduled for 19/11/2026 and 20/11/2026, followed by an interview. The selection process carries 400 marks, including 360 marks for the written exam and 40 marks for the interview.

Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) Result 2026
The Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) 2026 is being conducted by the University of Kota to determine the eligibility of candidates for the post of Assistant Professor in Rajasthan. Online applications will be accepted from 14 June 2026 to 15 July 2026, and the examination is scheduled to be held on 06 September 2026. Candidates must possess a Master's Degree with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) from a recognized university. The examination will consist of two papers carrying a total of 150 questions and 300 marks, with no negative marking. The Rajasthan SET certificate will be valid for a lifetime and can be used for Assistant Professor recruitment within the state of Rajasthan.
