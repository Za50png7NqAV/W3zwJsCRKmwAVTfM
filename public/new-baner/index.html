Indian Army NCC Special Entry Scheme 125 Course 2026
The Indian Army NCC Special Entry Scheme 125th Course (April 2027) notification has been released for 70 Short Service Commission (Non-Technical) vacancies for unmarried male NCC candidates, including 63 General and 07 Wards of Battle Casualties posts. Eligible candidates with a Bachelor's Degree, minimum 'B' Grade in NCC 'C' Certificate, and the required NCC service can apply online from 20/07/2026 to 20/08/2026. The selection process includes shortlisting, SSB Interview, medical examination, and merit list. Selected candidates will undergo 49 weeks of training at OTA Chennai and will be commissioned as Lieutenants in the Indian Army.

RPSC Physiotherapist Vacancy 2026
The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released the Physiotherapist Recruitment 2026 notification to fill 20 Physiotherapist (NSA) vacancies under the Medical and Health Department, Government of Rajasthan. The online application process will begin on 23 July 2026 and continue until 21 August 2026. Candidates who have passed Senior Secondary (10+2) and possess a Diploma in Physiotherapy from a recognized institution are eligible to apply. Applicants must also have a working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari Script and knowledge of Rajasthan Culture. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with age relaxation applicable as per Rajasthan Government rules. Interested candidates should read the official notification carefully and submit their applications before the last date.

MP Anganwadi Bharti 2026 Provisional List (अनंतिम सूची) Released
The Directorate of Women and Child Development, Madhya Pradesh has released the MP Anganwadi Recruitment 2026 Notification for the recruitment of 2,548 vacancies, including 781 Anganwadi Worker and 1,767 Anganwadi Assistant posts. Eligible female candidates who have passed 12th Class and are residents of the same Gram Panchayat (rural) or same Ward (urban) where the vacancy is available can apply online through the MP Online Selection Portal. The online application process will be conducted from 01 July 2026 to 13 July 2026, with corrections allowed until 15 July 2026. Candidates must be 18 to 35 years of age as on 01 January 2026 and pay an application fee of ₹100 + 18% GST.
