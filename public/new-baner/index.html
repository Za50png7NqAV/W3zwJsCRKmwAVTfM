IGCAR Trade Apprentice Vacancy 2026
The Indira Gandhi Centre for Atomic Research (IGCAR), Kalpakkam, under the Department of Atomic Energy (DAE), Government of India, has released the IGCAR Trade Apprentice Recruitment 2026 notification for 200 ITI Trade Apprentice vacancies under the Apprentices Act, 1961. Eligible ITI pass candidates in the relevant trades can submit their online applications from 20 July 2026 to 19 August 2026. The apprenticeship training will be conducted for one year at IGCAR, Kalpakkam, Tamil Nadu. Selected apprentices will receive a monthly stipend of ₹11,040 (for 2-year ITI) or ₹10,560 (for 1-year ITI). Selection will be based on ITI marks, followed by document verification and a medical examination.

RPSC Physiotherapist Vacancy 2026
The Rajasthan Public Service Commission (RPSC) has released the Physiotherapist Recruitment 2026 notification to fill 20 Physiotherapist (NSA) vacancies under the Medical and Health Department, Government of Rajasthan. The online application process will begin on 23 July 2026 and continue until 21 August 2026. Candidates who have passed Senior Secondary (10+2) and possess a Diploma in Physiotherapy from a recognized institution are eligible to apply. Applicants must also have a working knowledge of Hindi written in Devanagari Script and knowledge of Rajasthan Culture. The age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01 January 2027, with age relaxation applicable as per Rajasthan Government rules. Interested candidates should read the official notification carefully and submit their applications before the last date.

UP Anganwadi Worker Bharti 2026
UP Anganwadi Bharti 2026 is a recruitment drive conducted by the Child Development Services & Nutrition Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh, to fill Anganwadi Worker (कार्यकर्त्री) vacancies across various districts. UPSSSC Assistant Statistical Officer ASO Admit Card 2026
