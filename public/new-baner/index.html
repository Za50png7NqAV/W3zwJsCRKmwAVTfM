BPSC School Teacher TRE 4.0 Vacancy 2026 - 33,320 Posts
Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 notification for the recruitment of 32,388 School Teachers under the Education Department, Bihar. Vacancies are available for Primary (Classes 1–5), Middle (Classes 6–8), Secondary (Classes 9–10) and Higher Secondary (Classes 11–12) teachers. Eligible candidates can apply online from 01/09/2026 to 30/09/2026, with fee payment available from 01/09/2026 to 29/09/2026. Candidates should check the prescribed educational qualifications, subject-wise eligibility, age limit and other conditions before applying.

UP Police Home Guard Final Result 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Home Guard Recruitment 2025 has announced 41,424 vacancies for Home Guard (Male/Female) posts across all districts of the state. The online application process will run from 18 November 2025 to 17 December 2025. Candidates must have passed Class 10 (High School) from a recognized board and must be between 18 to 30 years of age as on 01 July 2025. The selection process includes Physical Standards Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET), where male candidates must complete 4.8 km in 28 minutes and female candidates must complete 2.4 km in 16 minutes. The application fee is ₹400 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹300 for SC/ST candidates. The complete district-wise vacancy list and PET/PST rules are provided in the official notification.

RRB Junior Engineer CEN 04/2026 Revised Exam Date
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts. A total of 4098 vacancies will be filled across various RRBs in India. Eligible candidates will be able to submit their applications online from 14/08/2026 (Tentative) to 13/09/2026 (Tentative). Selected candidates will receive an initial pay of ₹35,400 per month under Pay Level-6 (7th CPC). Applicants must be 18 to 33 years of age as on 01/01/2027, and the detailed notification will include post-wise eligibility, selection process, application fee, and other recruitment details. Candidates are advised to keep their Aadhaar details updated before applying online.
