Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 6th Admission 2027
Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) 2027 is conducted by the Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) for admission to Class VI in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas for the academic session 2027–28. Eligible candidates must be bona fide residents of the concerned district, studying in Class V during the 2026–27 session in a recognized school, and born between 01 May 2015 and 31 July 2017 (both dates inclusive). The online application process is free of cost, and the last date to apply is 31 July 2026. The entrance examination will be held on 28 November 2026, and selected students will receive free education, boarding, lodging, textbooks, and uniforms in the residential schools.

Patna High Court Assistant Recruitment 2026
The Patna High Court has released the Ex-Cadre Assistant Recruitment 2026 notification for 68 vacancies in Group-C posts. Eligible graduates with a 6-month Computer Application Certificate/Diploma can apply online from 28/07/2026 to 27/08/2026. The selection process includes a Written Examination, Computer Proficiency Test (CPT), and Interview. Interested candidates should carefully check the eligibility criteria and submit their online application before the last date.
