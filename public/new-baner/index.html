SBI Junior Associate Clerk Vacancy 2026
State Bank of India (SBI) has released the Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) Special Recruitment Drive 2026 notification to fill 1,538 backlog vacancies for SC, ST, and OBC categories across various States and Union Territories.
