UPSC Specialist & Assistant Public Prosecutor Vacancy 2026
UPSC has invited online applications under Advertisement No. 11/2026 for 212 posts including Specialist Grade III Assistant Professor, Assistant Editor, Specialist Grade-II, Assistant Public Prosecutor and Public Law Officer/Law Officer. RRB Junior Engineer CEN 04/2026 Exam Date
The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the Indicative Notification (CEN No. 04/2026) for the recruitment of Junior Engineer (JE), Depot Material Superintendent (DMS), and Chemical & Metallurgical Assistant (CMA) posts.
