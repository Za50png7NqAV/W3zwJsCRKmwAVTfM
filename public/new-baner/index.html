BOB Wealth Executive Vacancy 2026
Bank of Baroda has released a recruitment notification for 1,100 regular posts in various departments. The vacancies include 1,000 Wealth Executive posts in Wealth Management Services and 100 Credit Analyst–C&IC posts in Corporate & Institutional Credit. The Wealth Executive post requires graduation in any discipline along with one of the prescribed valid certifications such as IC 38 or specified NISM certifications, plus at least 2 years of relevant wealth-product sales experience. The Credit Analyst–C&IC post requires graduation, CA qualification, and at least 1 year of banking experience in a Scheduled Commercial Bank.

UPSSSC Computer Cadre (Computer Operator) Vacancy 2026
UPSSSC Computer Cadre Recruitment 2026 is being conducted for a total of 12 posts, including 11 Computer Operator Grade-A posts and 1 Assistant Computer Operator post. Eligible candidates must have a graduation degree along with a Diploma in Computer Science or NIELIT/DOEACC ‘O’ Level Certificate and the required experience in the field of Computer Applications. The online application process will be open from 07/09/2026 to 28/09/2026, while the last date for fee payment/correction is 05/10/2026. The application fee is ₹25 for all categories. Selection will be through the prescribed main examination, with candidates shortlisted on the basis of their PET-2025 normalized score.

MP ITI Admission 2026 process has started for admission in Government and Private ITI colleges of Madhya Pradesh. Candidates should check eligibility, important dates, fees, trade details and required documents before filling the application form.

UPSC CDS II Admit Card 2026
