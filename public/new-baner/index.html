UPESSC PRT Assistant Teacher Vacancy 2026
UPESSC has released Assistant Teacher Selection Exam 2026 (Advertisement No. 05/2026) for a total of 12,405 posts, including 11,508 Assistant Teacher posts in Urban Primary Schools and 897 Assistant Teacher Attached Primary posts in Non-Government Aided Schools. The online application process starts on 16/09/2026, with the written examination scheduled for 03 & 04 December 2026. Candidates must fulfill the prescribed educational qualifications, teacher training requirements and TET eligibility as applicable to the post. The written exam will consist of 120 objective-type questions carrying 360 marks, with 1 mark negative marking for each wrong/multiple answer.

UP Special TET Answer Key 2026
UPESSC has released the Special Teacher Eligibility Test (Special TET) 2026 under Advertisement No. 03/2026 for eligible teachers working at the Primary and Upper Primary levels, along with Special Educators (CWSN). The online application process is based on OTR, with registration starting from 05/09/2026. The examination is scheduled for 03/11/2026. The exam will consist of 150 MCQs carrying 150 marks, with a duration of 2 hours 30 minutes and no negative marking. Candidates should carefully check the prescribed eligibility, qualification, documents and other requirements before applying.

SSC CGL Tier-I Answer Key 2026
SSC CGL Recruitment 2026 notification has been released for 12,256 approximate vacancies on various Group B and Group C posts. Candidates having Bachelor Degree in any stream can apply online from 21 May 2026 to 22 June 2026. The recruitment includes posts like Assistant Section Officer, Income Tax Inspector, Auditor, Tax Assistant, CBI Sub Inspector, JSO and many more. Application fee is ₹100 for General/OBC/EWS while SC/ST/Female candidates are exempted. Tier-I exam will be conducted in August–September 2026.
