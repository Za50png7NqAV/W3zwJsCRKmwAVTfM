Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) Answer Key 2026
The Rajasthan State Eligibility Test (SET) 2026 is being conducted by the University of Kota to determine the eligibility of candidates for the post of Assistant Professor in Rajasthan. Online applications will be accepted from 14 June 2026 to 15 July 2026, and the examination is scheduled to be held on 06 September 2026. Candidates must possess a Master's Degree with at least 55% marks (50% for reserved categories) from a recognized university. The examination will consist of two papers carrying a total of 150 questions and 300 marks, with no negative marking. The Rajasthan SET certificate will be valid for a lifetime and can be used for Assistant Professor recruitment within the state of Rajasthan.

MPESB Nayab Tehsildar Vacancy 2026
MPESB Nayab Tehsildar Recruitment 2026 is a departmental limited competitive examination for recruitment to 73 Nayab Tehsildar posts. Eligible candidates must belong to the prescribed Revenue Department cadres and meet the required educational qualification, service, age and attempt criteria. The examination is scheduled to begin from 14/11/2026. The examination fee is ₹2,000/-, with applicable portal charges. Candidates should carefully read the official notification/rule book for detailed eligibility and other conditions.

MPESB Primary and Secondary Teachers MSPSTET Form 2026
MPESB has released the Teacher Eligibility Test 2026 for in-service Primary and Secondary Teachers. Eligible candidates can apply online from 21/08/2026 to 04/09/2026, while the application correction window will remain open until 09/09/2026. The examination will start from 12/10/2026. The application fee is ₹1,000 for all candidates, and the age limit is 18 to 40 years as on 01/01/2026.
