NVS Class 9th Admission Online Form 2027-28
Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti has released the notification for Class 9th Lateral Entry Selection Test 2027-28 for admission against vacant seats in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas. Online applications can be submitted from 02/09/2026 to 30/09/2026, and the Selection Test will be conducted on 10/04/2027. Candidates must be bona fide residents of the concerned district, studying Class VIII in the 2026-27 academic session in a Government/Government-recognized school of the same district, with a date of birth between 01/05/2012 and 31/07/2014. The test will consist of 100 objective questions for 100 marks from English, Hindi, Mathematics and General Science, with a duration of 2½ hours and no negative marking. The application process is free of cost, and final admission will be subject to document verification and availability of seats.

Uttar Pradesh Home Guard PET / PST Admit Card 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Home Guard Recruitment 2025 has announced 41,424 vacancies for Home Guard (Male/Female) posts across all districts of the state. The online application process will run from 18 November 2025 to 17 December 2025. Candidates must have passed Class 10 (High School) from a recognized board and must be between 18 to 30 years of age as on 01 July 2025. The selection process includes Physical Standards Test (PST) and Physical Efficiency Test (PET), where male candidates must complete 4.8 km in 28 minutes and female candidates must complete 2.4 km in 16 minutes. The application fee is ₹400 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹300 for SC/ST candidates. The complete district-wise vacancy list and PET/PST rules are provided in the official notification.
