UP Special TET Result 2026
UPESSC has released the Special Teacher Eligibility Test (Special TET) 2026 under Advertisement No. 03/2026 for eligible teachers working at the Primary and Upper Primary levels, along with Special Educators (CWSN). The online application process is based on OTR, with registration starting from 05/09/2026. The examination is scheduled for 03/11/2026. The exam will consist of 150 MCQs carrying 150 marks, with a duration of 2 hours 30 minutes and no negative marking. Candidates should carefully check the prescribed eligibility, qualification, documents and other requirements before applying.

UPESSC Principal Result 2026
The Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission (UPESSC) has released the Principal Recruitment 2026 Notification (Advertisement No. 02/2026) for 111 Principal posts in aided degree colleges across Uttar Pradesh. Eligible candidates holding a Ph.D. degree, 15 years of teaching/research experience, 10 research publications, and a minimum API score of 110 can apply online from 21 July 2026 to 20 August 2026. The written examination is scheduled for 24 September 2026, and the selected candidates will receive a Level-14 salary of ₹1,44,200–₹2,18,200 per month. Interested applicants should read the official notification carefully before submitting their online application.

SSC CPO CAPF SI Admit Card 2026
SSC has released the SSC CPO 2026 Notification for 1,871 Sub-Inspector (SI) posts in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs). Eligible candidates can apply online from 10/09/2026 to 30/09/2026. The recruitment includes Delhi Police SI (Male & Female), CAPF SI (GD), and CISF SI (Fire) posts. Candidates must meet the prescribed educational qualification, age limit, physical standards and medical requirements. The selection process includes Paper-I, PST/PET, Paper-II, Detailed Medical Examination/Review Medical Examination and Document Verification.
