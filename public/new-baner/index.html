Navodaya Vidyalaya Class 6th Admission 2027
Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) 2027 is conducted by the Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS) for admission to Class VI in Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalayas for the academic session 2027–28. Eligible candidates must be bona fide residents of the concerned district, studying in Class V during the 2026–27 session in a recognized school, and born between 01 May 2015 and 31 July 2017 (both dates inclusive). The online application process is free of cost, and the last date to apply is 31 July 2026. The entrance examination will be held on 28 November 2026, and selected students will receive free education, boarding, lodging, textbooks, and uniforms in the residential schools.

BPSSC Bihar Police ASI Operation Admit Card 2026
Bihar Police Radio Assistant Sub Inspector (Operation) Recruitment 2026 has been released by BPSSC for a total of 462 posts. RRB NTPC Graduate Level CEN 06/2025 CBT II Admit Card 2026
The Railway Recruitment Board (RRB) has announced the RRB NTPC Graduate Level Recruitment 2025 (CEN 06/2025) with around 5800 vacancies under the graduate category. Eligible candidates with a Bachelor’s degree can apply online for posts such as Station Master, Goods Guard, Senior Clerk cum Typist, Junior Accounts Assistant, Commercial Apprentice, and Traffic Assistant. The online application window will remain open from 21 October 2025 to 20 November 2025. The selection process includes CBT-1, CBT-2, Computer Based Aptitude Test (for Station Master & Traffic Assistant), Typing Skill Test (for Clerk/Typist posts), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. The salary ranges from ₹25,500 to ₹35,400 (Level-4 to Level-6 as per 7th CPC) along with allowances. Candidates must check the official notification for detailed eligibility, post-wise vacancies, exam pattern, and other important updates.
