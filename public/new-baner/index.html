BPSC School Teacher TRE 4.0 Vacancy 2026
Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 notification for the recruitment of 32,388 School Teachers under the Education Department, Bihar. Vacancies are available for Primary (Classes 1–5), Middle (Classes 6–8), Secondary (Classes 9–10) and Higher Secondary (Classes 11–12) teachers. Eligible candidates can apply online from 01/09/2026 to 30/09/2026, with fee payment available from 01/09/2026 to 29/09/2026. Candidates should check the prescribed educational qualifications, subject-wise eligibility, age limit and other conditions before applying.

Bank of Baroda Local Bank Officer LBO Vacancy 2026
Bank of Baroda (BOB) has released the Local Bank Officer Recruitment 2026 notification for 2,482 posts. Candidates aged 21 to 30 years as on 01/08/2026, with graduation in any discipline and at least 1 year of post-qualification experience as an Officer in a Scheduled Commercial Bank, can apply. Candidates must also be proficient in the local language of the State for which they apply. Online applications are open from 18/08/2026 to 07/09/2026.

MPESB Group 2 Sub Group 4 Patwari and Other Vacancy 2026
The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) has released the Group 2 Sub Group 4 Combined Recruitment Test 2026 notification to fill 2,106 vacancies across various government departments in Madhya Pradesh. The online application process begins on 04/08/2026 and will remain open until 18/08/2026, while application corrections can be made up to 23/08/2026. The recruitment examination is scheduled to start from 22/09/2026. Candidates possessing a Bachelor's Degree or other post-specific qualifications from a recognized university are eligible to apply, subject to the eligibility criteria prescribed for each post. Applicants are advised to read the official notification carefully before submitting their online application.
