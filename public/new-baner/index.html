Rajasthan Safai Karamchari Bharti 2026
The Rajasthan Contractual Safai Karamchari Recruitment 2026 notification has been released by the Local Self Government Department, Government of Rajasthan to fill 24,752 vacancies in various Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) across the state. Eligible candidates can submit their online applications from 15 August 2026 to 28 September 2026 through the official recruitment portal. There is no minimum educational qualification, but applicants must be Rajasthan residents and possess at least one year of sanitation work experience in a Rajasthan ULB. The selection of eligible candidates will be conducted through a lottery system, followed by document verification. Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before applying to ensure they meet all eligibility conditions.

SSC GD Constable Result 2026
The SSC GD Constable Recruitment 2026 has been announced for a total of 25,487 vacancies across various forces. The online application process will be conducted from 01/12/2025 to 31/12/2025. Candidates must have passed Class 10th (Matriculation) to be eligible. The selection process includes a Computer-Based Examination (CBT), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Physical Standard Test (PST), and Medical Examination. The application fee is ₹100 for General/OBC/EWS, while SC/ST, Female, and Ex-Servicemen candidates are exempted from the fee. Detailed force-wise vacancies, age limit, physical standards, and exam pattern are provided in the official notification.
