BOB Bank of Baroda Wealth Executive Vacancy 2026
Bank of Baroda has released a recruitment notification for 1,100 regular posts in various departments. The vacancies include 1,000 Wealth Executive posts in Wealth Management Services and 100 Credit Analyst–C&IC posts in Corporate & Institutional Credit. The Wealth Executive post requires graduation in any discipline along with one of the prescribed valid certifications such as IC 38 or specified NISM certifications, plus at least 2 years of relevant wealth-product sales experience. The Credit Analyst–C&IC post requires graduation, CA qualification, and at least 1 year of banking experience in a Scheduled Commercial Bank.

UPSSSC PET 2026 Exam Date
The Uttar Pradesh Subordinate Services Selection Commission (UPSSSC) has released the Preliminary Eligibility Test (PET) 2026 notification for candidates seeking future Group 'C' Government Jobs in Uttar Pradesh. Eligible candidates who have passed High School (Class 10) or an equivalent examination can submit their online applications from 03 August 2026 to 01 September 2026 through the official UPSSSC portal. The application correction window will remain open until 08 September 2026. The minimum age required is 18 years, while the maximum age is 40 years as on 01 July 2026, with age relaxation applicable as per government rules. PET 2026 is a qualifying examination and does not announce any specific vacancies or post names. Candidates qualifying in PET will be eligible to apply for various future UPSSSC Group 'C' recruitment notifications based on their PET score.

BPSC School Teacher TRE 4.0 Vacancy 2026
Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the TRE 4.0 Recruitment 2026 notification for the recruitment of 32,388 School Teachers under the Education Department, Bihar.
