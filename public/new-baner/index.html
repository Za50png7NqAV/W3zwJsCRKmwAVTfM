BPCL Non-Management Vacancy 2026
Bharat Petroleum Corporation Limited (BPCL) has released the Non-Management Recruitment 2026 notification for 154 vacancies in Mumbai Refinery and Kochi Refinery. The recruitment includes Process Technician (Chemical), Process Operator (Chemical), Technician/Operator (Mechanical), and Technician (Electrical) posts. Candidates with a full-time Diploma in the relevant engineering discipline along with 1 year of post-qualification experience or apprenticeship are eligible to apply. The online application process started on 29/07/2026 and will remain open until 13/08/2026. Selection will be based on a Computer Based Test (CBT), Skill/Proficiency Test (SPT), Document Verification, and Medical Examination. Selected candidates will receive a pay scale of ₹26,500–₹34,000 along with other BPCL allowances and benefits.

ISRO URSC Apprentice Vacancy 2026
The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has released the URSC Apprentice Recruitment 2026-27 notification for 410 Apprentice posts at the U R Rao Satellite Centre (URSC), Bengaluru. The recruitment includes Graduate Apprentice (B.E./B.Tech.), Technician Apprentice (Diploma), and Commercial Practice Apprentice positions for a one-year apprenticeship training. Eligible candidates who completed their qualifying degree or diploma in 2022, 2023, 2024, 2025, or 2026 can apply online through the NATS and UMANG portals. The online application process started on 29/07/2026, and the last date to submit the application is 28/08/2026. There is no application fee for any category. Selection will be based on academic performance and document verification.

ISRO Scientist / Engineer Vacancy 2026
The Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) has released the Scientist/Engineer 'SC' Recruitment 2026 notification to fill 92 vacancies across multiple engineering disciplines, including Electronics, Mechanical, Computer Science, Civil, Electrical, Refrigeration & Air Conditioning, and Architecture. Candidates holding a BE/B.Tech or equivalent degree with a minimum of 65% marks (or 6.84 CGPA) and a valid GATE 2025 or GATE 2026 score in the relevant discipline are eligible to apply. The online application process started on 28 July 2026 and will remain open until 17 August 2026. The selection process consists of GATE score-based shortlisting followed by an Interview, and selected candidates will receive a starting basic pay of ₹56,100 per month (Level-10 Pay Matrix) along with Central Government allowances and benefits. Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before submitting the online application.

Rajasthan Safai Karamchari Bharti 2026
The Rajasthan Contractual Safai Karamchari Recruitment 2026 notification has been released by the Local Self Government Department, Government of Rajasthan to fill 24,752 vacancies in various Urban Local Bodies (ULBs) across the state. Eligible candidates can submit their online applications from 15 August 2026 to 28 September 2026 through the official recruitment portal. There is no minimum educational qualification, but applicants must be Rajasthan residents and possess at least one year of sanitation work experience in a Rajasthan ULB. The selection of eligible candidates will be conducted through a lottery system, followed by document verification. Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully before applying to ensure they meet all eligibility conditions.
