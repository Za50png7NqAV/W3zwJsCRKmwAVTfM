RRB Group D / Level 1 CEN 09/2025 Admit Card 2026
Railway Recruitment Boards have released the indicative notice for Level-1 / Group D Recruitment under CEN 09/2025 to fill around 21997 vacancies across various RRB regions. These posts fall under Level-1 of the 7th Pay Commission with a starting pay of ₹18000 per month along with allowances. The online application process will begin on 21 January 2026 and will remain open till 20 February 2026 on the official RRB application portal. Candidates between 18 and 33 years of age as on 01 January 2026 who meet the prescribed educational qualifications will be eligible to apply. Complete details regarding post-wise eligibility selection process syllabus and medical standards will be available in the detailed notification published by the respective RRBs.

UPSC NDA II Exam Schedule 2026
UPSC NDA II Recruitment 2026 notification has been released for 394 posts in Army, Navy, Air Force and Naval Academy. Eligible unmarried male and female candidates can apply online from 20/05/2026 to 09/06/2026 through the UPSC website. Candidates must have passed 12th class, while PCM is mandatory for Navy and Air Force posts. The NDA II 2026 examination will be held on 13/09/2026. Selection will be based on Written Exam, SSB Interview and Medical Test.

UPSC CDS II Exam Schedule 2026
UPSC Combined Defence Services CDS II Recruitment 2026 notification has been released for 451 vacancies in Indian Army, Navy and Air Force academies including IMA, INA, AFA and OTA courses. Graduation pass candidates can apply online from 20 May 2026 to 09 June 2026 through the UPSC official website. The selection process includes Written Examination, SSB Interview and Medical Examination. The CDS II 2026 exam will be conducted on 13 September 2026.
