Indian Overseas Bank Security Guard Vacancy 2026
Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) has released the Security Guard Recruitment 2026 notification for 25 vacancies in Chennai/Tamil Nadu. The recruitment is exclusively for eligible Ex-Servicemen and Ex-Agniveers from the Army, Navy, or Air Force. Candidates must have passed 10th standard or equivalent, should not have passed Graduation, and must be able to speak, read, and write Tamil. The age limit is 18 to 26 years as on 01/08/2026, with applicable relaxation for Ex-Servicemen/Ex-Agniveers. The online application process is open from 25/08/2026 to 14/09/2026, and no application fee is required. Selection will be based on an Online Objective Test, Tamil Language Test, and Physical Fitness Test. The Physical Fitness Test includes Push-Ups, Sit-Ups, a 1 km Run, and Drill. Selected candidates will be placed in the prescribed subordinate staff pay scale, with the basic pay fixed based on their last drawn defence salary.

UPSC CAPF Assistant Commandant Result 2026
The CAPF Assistant Commandant 2026 Notification has been officially released for a total of 349 vacancies in BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, and SSB. The online application process started on 20/02/2026 and the last date to apply is 12/03/2026 (till 6:00 PM). The written examination will be conducted on 19/07/2026. Candidates must hold a Bachelor’s Degree from a recognized university. The age limit is 20 to 25 years as on 01 August 2026, with age relaxation applicable as per government rules for reserved categories. The selection process includes Written Examination, Physical Standards Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET), Interview/Personality Test, and Medical Examination. Both male and female candidates are eligible to apply.
