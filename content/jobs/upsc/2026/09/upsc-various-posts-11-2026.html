Assistant Editor 02 Bachelor's Degree in Journalism or Mass Communication; OR Bachelor's Degree with Economics, Statistics or English as a subject + at least 1-year Diploma in Journalism/Mass Communication + 3 years' experience in editing and publishing works.
Specialist Grade-II (Junior Scale) – Anaesthesiology 01 Recognized Medical Qualification + Post-Graduate Degree/Diploma in Anaesthesiology such as MD/MS (Anaesthesiology), D.A. or equivalent qualification + 3 years' experience after PG Degree OR 5 years' experience after PG Diploma.
Specialist Grade-II (Junior Scale) – Pediatrics 01 Recognized Medical Qualification + Post-Graduate Degree/Diploma in Paediatrics such as MD (Paediatrics) or D.C.H. + 3 years' experience after PG Degree OR 5 years' experience after PG Diploma.
Assistant Public Prosecutor 140 Degree in Law from a recognized University/Institute + 3 years' experience at the Bar.
Public Law Officer / District Litigation Officer / Law Officer 04 Bachelor's Degree in Law (Professional or Integrated) from a university established by law in India + 2 years' actual practice at the Bar, certified by the concerned competent judicial authority.
