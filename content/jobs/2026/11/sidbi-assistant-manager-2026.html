SIDBI Assistant Manager & Manager Recruitment 2026 – Post Wise Vacancy & Eligibility
Post Name Total Posts Eligibility Criteria
Assistant Manager – General Stream 53
  • Graduation in Commerce, Economics, Mathematics, Statistics, Business Administration or Engineering with 60% marks (SC/ST/PwBD: 50%).
  • OR CA, CS, CMA, CFA or 2-year Full-Time MBA/PGDM.
  • Generally, 2 years of relevant Officer-level experience or 3 years of equivalent Class III experience.
  • Eligible CA and Full-Time MBA/PGDM candidates are exempted from the experience requirement.
Assistant Manager – Hindi Stream 2
  • Master's Degree in Hindi with 50% marks and English studied at Graduation level for at least 2 years.
  • OR Master's Degree in English with 50% marks and Hindi studied at Graduation level for at least 2 years.
  • Alternatively, Graduation with Hindi and English subjects plus a 1-year PG Diploma in Hindi-English Translation.
  • Minimum 2 years of relevant translation or Official Language work experience.
Assistant Manager – Legal Stream 3
  • Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB).
  • Minimum 50% marks (SC/ST/PwBD: 45%).
  • Must be enrolled as an Advocate with the Bar Council of India.
  • Minimum 2 years of relevant Legal, Recovery or Commercial Contract Management experience.
Assistant Manager – Systems / IT 5
  • B.E./B.Tech. in Engineering/Technology with 60% marks (SC/ST/PwBD: 55%).
  • OR MCA with prescribed marks.
  • Minimum 2 years of relevant IT / Systems Officer experience.
  • Relevant areas include Computer Science, IT, Electronics, AI/ML, Cyber Security, Networking and Infrastructure.
Manager – Hindi Stream 1
  • Master's Degree in Hindi or English with prescribed language requirements.
  • OR Graduation with Hindi and English subjects plus a 1-year PG Diploma in Translation.
  • Minimum 5 years of relevant post-qualification experience in Hindi-English Translation or Official Language work.
Manager – Legal Stream 3
  • Bachelor's Degree in Law (LLB).
  • Minimum 50% marks (SC/ST/PwBD: 45%).
  • Must be enrolled as an Advocate with the Bar Council of India.
  • Minimum 5 years of relevant post-qualification experience as a Law Officer.
  • Experience in Legal, Recovery or Commercial Contract Management.
Manager – Systems / IT 1
  • B.E./B.Tech. in Engineering/Technology with 60% marks (SC/ST/PwBD: 55%).
  • OR MCA with prescribed marks.
  • Minimum 5 years of relevant post-qualification IT / Systems Officer experience.
  • Specialisation in AI/ML, Full Stack Development, IT Security, Infrastructure or Network Management.
  • Additional specific experience requirements apply according to the selected skill area.
