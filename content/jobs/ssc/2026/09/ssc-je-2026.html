Post Name Total Post Eligibility
Junior Engineer (JE) 1,748 Candidates must possess a Degree or a Three-Year Diploma in the relevant Engineering discipline from a recognized University, Institute, or Board. The relevant disciplines include Civil Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and other disciplines specified for the respective Junior Engineer post/organization.

For certain posts, candidates with a Diploma qualification must also have the prescribed relevant work experience in Planning, Execution and Maintenance of Engineering works.

Note: Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for additional eligibility details.
