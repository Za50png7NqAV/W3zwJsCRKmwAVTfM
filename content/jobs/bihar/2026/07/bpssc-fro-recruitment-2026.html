BPSSC Forest Range Officer (FRO) Recruitment 2026 – Eligibility Details
Post Name Total Posts Eligibility (Qualification + PET/PST)
Forest Range Officer (FRO) 16
  • Educational Qualification: Bachelor's Degree from a recognized University in Agriculture, Forestry, Botany, Zoology, Veterinary Science, Chemistry, Physics, Mathematics, Statistics, Geology, Engineering or equivalent with at least one prescribed subject.
  • PST (Physical Standard Test):
    • Height (Male): 163 cm (Gen/BC/EBC/SC), 152.5 cm (ST)
    • Height (Female): 150 cm (Gen/BC/EBC/SC), 145 cm (ST)
    • Chest (Male Only): 79 cm (Unexpanded), 84 cm (Expanded) with minimum 5 cm expansion.
    • Chest measurement is Not Applicable for Female candidates.
  • PET (Physical Efficiency Test):
    • Male: Walk 25 Km within 4 Hours.
    • Female: Walk 14 Km within 4 Hours.
  • Selection Process: Written Examination → Interview → PET/PST → Medical Examination.

Note: For detailed eligibility criteria and other conditions, candidates are strongly advised to read the official notification carefully before applying.
