Company Commander 65
  • Must be a trained male, female, or third-gender Home Guard who has completed at least 5 years of service/duty in the Bihar Home Guard organization.
  • Must have passed a Graduation examination from a recognized university or an equivalent examination recognized by the State Government by 01 August 2026.
  • The candidate must have at least 10 years of service remaining as on the date of consideration for appointment (cut-off date: 01 August 2026).
