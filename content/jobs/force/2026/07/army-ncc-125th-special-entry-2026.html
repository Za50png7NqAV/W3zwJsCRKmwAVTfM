Post Name Total Posts Educational Qualification
Short Service Commission (Non-Technical) – NCC Special Entry (Men) 63 Bachelor's Degree or equivalent qualification from a recognized University. Candidates must have served at least 2/3 years in the Senior Division/Wing of NCC and possess a valid NCC 'C' Certificate with minimum 'B' Grade. Final-year students are also eligible, subject to completion of Graduation before commencement of the course.

Short Service Commission (Non-Technical) – Wards of Battle Casualties 07 Bachelor's Degree or equivalent qualification from a recognized University. Final-year students may also apply. NCC 'C' Certificate is not required for Wards of Battle Casualties. Candidates must fulfill all other eligibility conditions prescribed in the notification.

Note: For detailed eligibility criteria, candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully.
