Bank Medical Officer (BMO-II) 35 Educational Qualification: MBBS degree from a recognized University/College approved by the National Medical Commission (NMC) / State Medical Council.

Experience:
  • MBBS Holders: Minimum 5 years of experience as a General Practitioner.
