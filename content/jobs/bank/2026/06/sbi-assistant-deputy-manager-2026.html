SBI SCO (Law) Recruitment 2026 – Post-wise Vacancy & Eligibility
Post Name Total Posts Eligibility
Assistant Manager (Law) 20 Graduate with an LLB (Law Degree) from a recognized university. Must be enrolled as an Advocate with the Bar Council. Candidates with a 5-year Integrated Law Course may be given preference. Minimum 2 years of experience as a practicing Advocate, or as a Law Officer in the Legal Department of a Scheduled Commercial Bank, or a combination of both. (Experience must be obtained after enrolment with the Bar Council.)
Deputy Manager (Law) 29 (27 Regular + 2 Backlog) Graduate with an LLB (Law Degree) from a recognized university. Must be enrolled as an Advocate with the Bar Council. Candidates with a 5-year Integrated Law Course may be given preference. Minimum 4 years of experience as a practicing Advocate, or as a Law Officer in the Legal Department of a Scheduled Commercial Bank, or a combination of both. (Experience must be obtained after enrolment with the Bar Council.)
