UPSSSC Livestock Extension Officer Recruitment 2026 – Post & Eligibility Details
Post Name Total Posts Eligibility / Educational Qualification
Livestock Extension Officer
(General & Special Selection)		 1,251 Candidates must have appeared in UPSSSC PET 2025 and must have been issued a PET 2025 Score Card by the Commission.

Candidates must have passed Intermediate (10+2) with Biology from the Uttar Pradesh Board of High School and Intermediate Education or an equivalent examination recognized by the Government.

Candidates must also have passed a Two-Year Livestock Extension Diploma Course from a recognized Veterinary University / Veterinary College.

Knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari Script is also required.

Note: Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility criteria and other requirements.
