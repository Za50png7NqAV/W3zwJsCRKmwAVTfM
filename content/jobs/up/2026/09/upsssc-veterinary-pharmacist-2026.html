Veterinary Pharmacist 1308
  • Must have passed Intermediate (12th) Examination with Biology from the Uttar Pradesh Board of High School and Intermediate Education or an equivalent examination recognized by the Government.
  • Must possess a 2 years 3 months Diploma in Veterinary Pharmacy from a University/Institution established by law in India.
  • Must be registered with the competent Council/Institution, wherever applicable.
  • Must have knowledge of Hindi in Devanagari Script.
  • The candidate must have appeared in UPSSSC PET-2025 and must have been issued a PET-2025 Score Card by the Commission.
Note: For detailed eligibility, educational qualification, age limit, reservation and other conditions, candidates must read the official recruitment notification carefully before applying.
