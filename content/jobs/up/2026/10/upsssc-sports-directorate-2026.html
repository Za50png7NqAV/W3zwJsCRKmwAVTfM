UPSSSC Sports Directorate 108

Must meet the UPSSSC PET-2025 eligibility requirements.

  • Grounds Man: High School/equivalent + practical grounds/court maintenance experience.
  • Deputy Sports Officer: Graduation + relevant Coaching Diploma + 2 years coaching experience.
  • Assistant Coach: Graduation + relevant Coaching Diploma + 2 years coaching experience.
  • Life Guard: High School/equivalent + 3 years practical Life Guard experience.
  • Preferential Qualification: 1 year sports competition experience, Physical Education Diploma/Certificate, 2 years Territorial Army service, or NCC ‘B’ Certificate.
  • Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility criteria before applying.
