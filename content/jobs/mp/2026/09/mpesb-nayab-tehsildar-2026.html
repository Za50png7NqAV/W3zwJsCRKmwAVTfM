MPESB Nayab Tehsildar Recruitment 2026 Short Details
Post Name Total Posts Eligibility / Educational Qualification
Nayab Tehsildar 73
  • Candidate must be working in the eligible Clerical Service or Patwari / Revenue Inspector Cadre of the Madhya Pradesh Revenue Department.
  • Candidate must have completed the prescribed minimum service period for appearing in the departmental limited competitive examination.
  • Must possess a Bachelor's Degree from a recognized university in Arts, Science (including Technical and Engineering), Commerce or Agriculture.
  • The prescribed minimum educational qualification must have been completed on or before the date of application.
  • Maximum age should not be more than 55 years as on 01 January 2026.
  • A candidate is not permitted to appear in the examination more than 3 times.
Note: For detailed eligibility criteria, candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully.
