MPESB Police Constable Driver Recruitment 2026 Short Details
Post Name Total Posts Eligibility
Constable (Driver) – District Police Force 800 Male candidates who have passed Class 10 or Class 12/equivalent from a recognized board or institution, as prescribed in the official rulebook. ST candidates may be eligible with Class 8 or equivalent qualification under the specified provisions. A heavy vehicle driving licence is mandatory, and candidates must qualify the prescribed practical driving test.
Constable (Driver) – Special Armed Force 200 Male candidates who meet the prescribed educational qualification and possess a heavy vehicle driving licence. Candidates must also qualify the prescribed practical driving test and fulfil the other eligibility conditions mentioned in the official rulebook.
Total 1,000 For additional eligibility criteria, candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully.
