Course Name / Class Eligibility
Class VI Admission Students aged 10 to 12 years as on 31 March 2027. Date of Birth must be between 01 April 2015 and 31 March 2017. Both boys and girls are eligible.
Class IX Admission Students aged 13 to 15 years as on 31 March 2027. Date of Birth must be between 01 April 2012 and 31 March 2014. Candidates must have passed Class VIII from a recognised school at the time of admission. Girls are eligible subject to vacancy availability.
