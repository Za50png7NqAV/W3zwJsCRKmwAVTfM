UP Special TET 2026 Short Details
Post Name Educational Qualification / Eligibility
Special Educator (CWSN) – Primary Level (Class 1–5)
  • Must be working as a Special Educator (CWSN) on a contract/daily-wage basis in a primary-level school.
  • Must have passed a Graduation degree.
  • Must have passed D.Ed. in Special Education or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI).
  • Must have an RCI Registration Certificate with an Active CRR Number.
Special Educator (CWSN) – Upper Primary Level (Class 6–8)
  • Must be working as a Special Educator (CWSN) on a contract/daily-wage basis in an upper-primary-level school.
  • Must have passed a Graduation degree.
  • Must have passed B.Ed. in Special Education or an equivalent qualification recognized by the Rehabilitation Council of India (RCI).
  • Must have an RCI Registration Certificate with an Active CRR Number.
Note: Candidates are advised to read the official notification carefully for detailed eligibility and other applicable requirements.
